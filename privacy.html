Privacy Policy

Shorts Tools · Effective October 12, 2026

Who uses this app

Shorts Tools is a private tool used by a single operator to manage the operator's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to other users.

Data accessed

With the channel owner's explicit consent through Google OAuth, the app accesses:

  • YouTube channel and video information of the operator's own channels (YouTube Data API, read-only scope);
  • YouTube Analytics reports for those channels (YouTube Analytics API, read-only scope);
  • permission to upload videos to those channels (YouTube Data API upload scope).

How data is used

Data is used only to upload and schedule the operator's videos and to analyze the performance of the operator's own videos for planning. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and retention

OAuth tokens and downloaded analytics are stored only on the operator's own computer. They can be deleted at any time by removing the local files. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Shorts Tools' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
